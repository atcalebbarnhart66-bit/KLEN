The Kansas Commission on Peace Officers' Standards and Training (CPOST) has
finalized updates to its de-escalation training requirements, which take
effect for the upcoming certification cycle. Agencies across the state will
need to incorporate the revised curriculum into both academy training and
annual in-service hours.

## What's changing

The revised standard expands scenario-based training and adds a
dedicated block on communicating with individuals experiencing a
behavioral health crisis. Three changes stand out for training
coordinators:

1. A minimum of four scenario-based hours per certification cycle, up from two.
2. A new module addressing crisis intervention alongside existing CIT programming.
3. Updated reporting requirements for agencies that contract out training delivery.

> "The goal isn't more paperwork — it's making sure every officer in Kansas
> has had hands-on practice with the situations most likely to escalate,"
> said one regional training coordinator familiar with the rollout.

## Timeline for agencies

Most departments will have a full training year to comply. Smaller
agencies that rely on regional training centers should confirm seat
availability early, since demand is expected to be high in the first two
quarters after the standard takes effect.

### Recommended next steps

- Review your agency's current in-service hour tracking against the new minimums.
- Contact your regional training center to reserve scenario-based training slots.
- Update field training officer (FTO) checklists to reflect the crisis intervention module.

For agencies using an outside vendor for de-escalation training, confirm
the vendor's curriculum has been cross-walked against the updated CPOST
standard before scheduling sessions.

```
Example in-service hour tracking snippet:
  De-escalation (scenario-based): 4 hrs  [required]
  Crisis intervention module:     2 hrs  [required]
  Legal update:                   2 hrs
```

Departments with questions about how the update applies to reserve or
part-time officers should route them through their normal CPOST liaison
channel.
