About KLEN

The Kansas Law Enforcement Network (KLEN) is an independent network connecting Kansas law enforcement professionals. KLEN is not an official government website and is not affiliated with, operated by, or endorsed by any law enforcement agency, municipality, county, or the State of Kansas.

Privacy

This site collects no personal data and sets no cookies. Messages sent from the Contact page are composed and sent directly by your own email application — nothing is transmitted to or stored on this website.
